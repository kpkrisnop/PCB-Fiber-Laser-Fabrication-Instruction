## Materials

- FR1 or FR4 copper board
- UV-curable solder mask in two colours: a light one under (silkscreen) and a dark one on top (mask), e.g. white then green
- Thick black paper for the laser jig
- Isopropyl alcohol (IPA) and lint-free wipes
- Via rivets (optional), to join top and bottom

## Machines and tools

- Fiber laser
- CNC router
- OD6+ 1064 nm goggles and a fume extractor
- UV light for curing
- Silkscreen mesh for spreading the solder mask
- Fine sandpaper
- Drill bits, 0.6–1.5 mm in 0.1 mm steps
- Cutting bit, 2.0 mm
- Four 0.8 mm alignment pins
- Four 3D-printed clamps, to hold the copper down
- Spoilboard: a sacrificial MDF board fixed to the CNC bed
- Multimeter
